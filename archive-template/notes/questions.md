# Open Questions

This is your research to-do list: things you want to pin down, and places where sources disagree. Add a `## Q:` entry for each question, with one `- ` line per field. The example below is inside a comment: copy it, remove the comment markers, and fill it in.

A question goes through up to four stages, all in the same entry:

1. **Question:** something you don't know. Just the heading and the first three lines.
2. **Working answer:** your best guess so far. Add `id:`, `guess:`, `basis:` (why you think so) and `verify:` (what would settle it). The `id:` is a code starting `h-`; a tool can make one for you, or you can make up ten characters yourself.
3. **Build on it:** you can enter the guess on a person's record as a relationship marked `status: hypothesis`, so you can keep researching past it. It's drawn as a guess and left out of anything you share.
4. **Settle it:** a source says it (file the source; its claim carries the fact), or the pieces add up (write the reasoning as a proof-argument source), or it's wrong (take the guess out and note `ruled out h-...` under `context:`). Then set the status to `answered` (or `resolved`; they mean the same) with the link that settled it, or to `closed (not pursuing)`.

A guess only you make is a working answer. Anything someone else says (a family story, an index entry, another researcher's tree) is a source, filed with a low-confidence claim.
