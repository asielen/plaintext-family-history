# Open Questions

This is your research to-do list: things you want to pin down, and conflicts between
sources. Add a `## Q:` entry per question, one `- ` line per field. The example below
is commented out - copy it, uncomment it, and fill it in.

A question climbs one ladder, all in the same entry:

1. **Question** - something you do not know. Just the heading and the first three lines.
2. **Working answer** - your best guess so far. Add `id:`, `guess:`, `basis:` (why you
   think so) and `verify:` (what would settle it). The `id:` is a code starting `h-`;
   a tool can make one for you, or roll ten characters yourself.
3. **Build on it** - you may enter the guess on a person's record as a relationship
   marked `status: hypothesis`, so you can keep researching past it. It is drawn as a
   guess and left out of anything you share.
4. **Settle it** - a source says it (file the source; its claim carries the fact), or
   the pieces add up (write the reasoning as a proof-argument source), or it is wrong
   (take the guess out and note `ruled out h-...` under `context:`). Then set the
   status to `answered` (or `resolved` - they mean the same) with the link that
   settled it, or `closed (not pursuing)`.

A guess only you make is a working answer. Anything someone else says - a family
story, an index entry, another researcher's tree - is a source, filed with a
low-confidence claim.
