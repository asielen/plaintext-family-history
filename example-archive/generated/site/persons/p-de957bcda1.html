Biography

Thomas Edward Hartley was born about 1840 in New York 1, the son of Caleb Comstock Hartley and Chastina Augusta Reed; in 1850 he was living with them in Easton, Washington County, New York 3 4.

He spent most of his adult life in Fairview, Kansas. He married Margaret A. Cole there early in 1871 2, and the 1880 census records him as a bookkeeper for the Plains Junction Railroad, living with Margaret and their daughters Ethel and Frances 1. In 1882 he was a member of the Fairview Masonic Lodge 5.
