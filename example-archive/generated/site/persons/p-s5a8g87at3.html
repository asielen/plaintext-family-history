Susan Cole

Biography

Susan Cole lived near the Hartleys in Fairview. She may have been an older sister of Margaret A. Cole: Margaret was married "at the residence of the bride's sister" 1. No record says so, so the tie is entered on her record as a guess (`status: hypothesis`) and followed while it is checked (notes/questions.md).
