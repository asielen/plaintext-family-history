Biography

James Robert Bradford was born on 15 March 1955 in Fairview, Breton County, Kansas, the son of George Arthur Bradford and Edith Louise Bradford, born Hartley 1. He graduated from Kansas State University in 1977 with a degree in history 2.

Returning to Fairview, James taught history at Fairview Consolidated High School for twenty years and then served as its principal until he retired in 2012 2. On 14 June 1979 he married Carol Anne Simmons in Fairview 2; their son Alex was born a few years later 2.

He died on 3 November 2015 in Topeka, Kansas 2.
