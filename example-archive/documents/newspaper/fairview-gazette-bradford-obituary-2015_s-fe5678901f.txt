JAMES ROBERT BRADFORD, 1955-2015
[Transcription of an obituary - FICTIONAL DOCUMENT for the example archive]
[Fairview Gazette, 7 November 2015, page 4]

James Robert "Jim" Bradford, 60, of Fairview died Tuesday, November 3, 2015, at a
Topeka hospital.

Jim was born March 15, 1955, in Fairview to George and Edith (Hartley) Bradford. He
graduated from Fairview High School in 1973 and from Kansas State University in 1977
with a degree in history. He taught history at Fairview Consolidated High School for
twenty years and then served as its principal until he retired in 2012.

He married Carol Simmons on June 14, 1979. Survivors include his wife, Carol, and their
son, Alex, both of Fairview.
