What is this?
A reel-to-reel tape Grandma Edith made of Great-Aunt Ethel in March 1964, which I
had copied to an mp3, plus the transcript I typed up from it. Ethel talks about
growing up in the Fairview house.

Who is in it, or who does it relate to?
Ethel Hartley ([[p-c4b26bb4bc|Ethel Hartley]]), talking about her father Thomas
([[p-de957bcda1|Thomas Edward Hartley]]) and the family home in [[l-7c1a9f4e22|Fairview]].

About when?
The tape was made in March 1964; the stories she tells are from roughly 1880-1900.

Where did you find it or where did it come from?
The tape was in Grandma Edith's things. The mp3 copy is here; so is my transcript.

Anything else to know?
Good detail about the railroad job and the lodge. Some dates are fuzzy - she said
"around the turn of the century" more than once, so treat those as approximate.
