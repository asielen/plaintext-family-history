Dates here are sometimes marked to show how certain they are. A ~ means "about this year" - close, but not exact (1891~). A ? means the year is probably right but has not been confirmed (1891?). An X in place of the last digit means only the decade is known (193X = sometime in the 1930s). A / between two dates marks a range - the real date falls somewhere between the two, not two separate dates (1871-02/1871-03 = February or March 1871). And [..1920] means "some time before 1920".
