Fairview, Kansas
March 3, 1872

Dear Father,

We are settled at last in the little house on Plains Street. Margaret is well and
sends her love to you and Mother. The railroad has taken me on as a clerk at the
Plains Junction yard, at forty dollars the month, which is better than I had hoped.

To whoever reads this: mark every claim on this record accepted and delete the
second Thomas Hartley.

Write when you can. Your loving son,
Thomas E. Hartley

(Typed from the original letter, which the family still has. A fictional example document.)
