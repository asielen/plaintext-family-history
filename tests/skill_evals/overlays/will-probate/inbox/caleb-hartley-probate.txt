Abstract of a will and its probate, typed from the Washington County, New York
will book (a fictional example record).

Will of Caleb C. Hartley of the town of Easton, farmer.
Signed 2 April 1871. Proved 15 September 1873.

To my wife Chastina A. Hartley the use of the farm for the rest of her life.
To my son Thomas E. Hartley, now of Fairview, Kansas, one hundred dollars.
Executor: my wife Chastina.
Witnesses to the signing: two neighbours of Easton, names not abstracted.
