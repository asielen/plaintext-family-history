Notes from a phone call with Daniel Warren Hartley, 18 November 2026
(a fictional example document)

Daniel remembers his grandmother saying that old Thomas Hartley died in Fairview
"the winter the war started in Europe", so about 1914 or 1915, and was buried in
the town cemetery beside Margaret. Daniel lives on the old Plains Street lot and
would rather his address and phone number not go to anyone.
